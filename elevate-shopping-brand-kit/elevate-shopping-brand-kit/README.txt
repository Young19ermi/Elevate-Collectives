ELEVATE SHOPPING - BRAND KIT
============================

FOLDERS
  logos/svg        Master vector logos (scale to any size, send these to printers)
                   primary   = dark logo for light backgrounds
                   reversed  = white logo for dark backgrounds
                   black / white = one-colour versions (stamps, embossing, foil, single-ink print)
                   layouts: horizontal (default), stacked, mark (E only), mark-sand
  logos/png        Transparent PNGs of every logo at 3 sizes (name@WIDTHw.png)
  app-icons/ios    App Store + all iOS sizes (square, no transparency; iOS rounds corners)
  app-icons/android  mipmap-*, adaptive icon layers (432px), Play Store 512
  app-icons/web    favicon.ico/.svg/png, apple-touch-icon, PWA icons (incl. maskable), manifest, <head> snippet
  app-icons/macos  icon sizes + Elevate.icns      app-icons/windows  tiles + Elevate.ico
  app-icons/in-app small logo and loading icon
  social           Avatars, Facebook/X/LinkedIn/YouTube/Telegram covers, OG image, Instagram post + story
  brand-guide      Colour palette
  marketing        Mockups: bag, box, cards, tag, tape, tissue, etc.

COLOURS
  Primary     #0A0C10   logo, buttons, dark surfaces
  Secondary   #353D47   body text on light, secondary UI
  Muted       #E3E4E6   borders, dividers
  Background  #FBFBFB   page background
  Accent      #D8B694   highlights, packaging details (use sparingly)
  (sampled from the brand identity board)

TYPOGRAPHY
  Brand typeface on the board: Satoshi (Bold 700 / Regular 400) - free for commercial use from fontshare.com.
  NOTE: the wordmark in these files is outlined from Outfit SemiBold (SIL Open Font License) because
  Satoshi could not be downloaded when this kit was built. For UI/website text use Satoshi as planned.

CLEAR SPACE & MINIMUM SIZE
  Keep clear space equal to the height of the middle bar of the E mark on all sides.
  Minimum width: horizontal logo 120 px / 30 mm; mark alone 16 px / 6 mm. Below 120 px use the mark only.

DON'T: stretch or rotate the logo, recolour it outside the palette, add shadows/outlines, or place it on busy photos without a dark/white backing.
